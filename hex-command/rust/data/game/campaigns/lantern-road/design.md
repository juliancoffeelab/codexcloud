# The Lantern Road

Nera’s patrol investigates an extinguished ferry lantern, follows abducted villagers along an occupied road, and confronts the Bellwarden’s machinery for raising the dead.

## The Silent Crossing

- Begin with Nera, Ivo, Oren and an archer; finite scouting contacts reinforce them instead of recruitment. Oren recognizes unnatural phenomena without knowing their cause.
- Cross by the island’s fords, confront the skeletons, visit the ferryman’s house, then reclaim both eastern villages and defeat their released spiders. Animals act independently of the staged skeleton encounter.
- Crossing the eastern ford by turn 15 saves the ferryman; later arrivals find his letter. Both routes continue, but rescue supplies six coins next chapter. An optional bear detour yields a transferable quarry hammer.

## Beyond the crossing

- Establish a camp and discover Sella’s hidden village; her account reveals the captive rescue. Escort the rescued group back to safety before Nera leaves through the northern pass.
- The Tollkeeper waits beside his keep until the patrol sees him or the guard, then moves and recruits freely. Defeating him is optional and prevents his return; clearing every enemy is unnecessary. His death prompts relief tempered by urgency.
- Entering the swamps wakes Edrin and his faction; income accumulates beforehand. Oren recognizes his former friend if alive. Edrin’s healing bottle can be recovered, but defeating him is optional.
- On turn 10, unrescued captives start toward the northern pass; losing them there defeats the chapter. Rescue transfers the existing group and overrides its march. Finish by round 30; the first bell on round 26 carries consequences forward. Disclose danger through discoveries, without exposing private schedules in hints.

## The bell and the graves

- Defeat the Bellwarden to win; rescue and the northern monastery are optional. Holding the monastery with a mage for three turns clears the fog. Victory opens the road without ending the wider lich threat.
- Starting on round 6, the bell consumes captives at one graveyard every six rounds, north then east then south. Each site fires once and raises three enemies; freed captives must leave the marks. A late arrival finds the northern ritual already completed.
- A surviving Tollkeeper returns with a finite patrol on round 5. Western reinforcements arrive on round 10 because of the runner sent after the village rescue. Bellwarden dialogue responds to actual sightings and rescues.
