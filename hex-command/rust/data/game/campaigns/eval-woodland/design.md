# One Voice in the Briars

Ash-Muzzle, a Wildkeeper of the independent valley packs, wants to become their shared leader. Chief Merek uses revenge for his brother’s death at Yara’s grove to justify occupying their feeding grounds. The conflict stays within one valley and exactly three battles.
The Feeding Trail wins a den’s pledge by removing an occupying patrol. Human ownership of its village clears two stands and brings a reserve; recapture restores the woods. Conditional aftermath distinguishes recovery, unrecovered cutting and victory before cutting.
Old Root uses the newly opened migration trail to reach a marsh causeway. Ash can claim the grove during battle to recruit thorncallers early; defeating the garrison secures arrival and the grove’s alliance. This turns military success into an actual woodland coalition.
The valley mouth is a positional fight across a river bowl. A slow, pierce-resistant treant can absorb the bow line while thornshields hold a narrow forest shelf and wolves flank it. The southern marsh ford offers a longer approach. Defeating Merek’s whole force ends his occupation; the packs and grove-dwellers acclaim Ash and withdraw together.
The campaign has no story fork. Grove recruitment and village restoration offer useful battlefield decisions without withholding the required ending. A conditional discovery in Merek’s camp adds context to his grievance but is unnecessary for victory. Dialogue accounts for fallen companions; veterans, promotions and their equipment use ordinary carryover.
All mechanics, units and artwork already exist. Terrain, ownership, conditional spawning, recruitment unlocks, positional enemy groups and durable flags carry the campaign. No engine changes are required. Native validation and simulation establish the checked scope; live UI play remains unverified.
