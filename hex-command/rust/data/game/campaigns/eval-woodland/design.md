# One Voice in the Briars

Ash-Muzzle, a Wildkeeper of the independent valley packs, wants to become their shared leader. Chief Merek uses revenge for his brother’s death at Yara’s grove to justify occupying their feeding grounds. The conflict stays within one valley and exactly three battles.
The Feeding Trail emerges from connected woods, crosses a boggy creek and wins a den’s pledge by removing the pasture patrol. Human ownership of its village clears two stands and brings a reserve; recapture restores the woods. Conditional aftermath distinguishes recovery, unrecovered cutting and victory before cutting.
Old Root uses the opened migration trail to reach a visible dry causeway through an irregular wetland, with a branch climbing to the grove. A held far-bank firing line makes the grove’s nearby keep useful: Ash can claim the grove to recruit thorncallers early. Defeating the garrison guarantees the alliance, Pip’s arrival and a protective bark mantle for Ash. The early claim stays optional.
The valley mouth is an open opposing-force battle. Merek brings a mobile veteran household, including armor, heavy impact weapons and a healer. A treant can absorb the marksman’s arrows, but cannot answer every threat. Shields protect the thorncallers while wolves seek the healer; local forest and hills offer cover, and the southern marsh ford remains a flank. Defeating Merek’s whole army ends his occupation; den and grove acclaim Ash, then withdraw together.
