# The Split-Reef Command

Orra wants command of a divided merfolk watch. A woodland practitioner has seized their freshwater spring after builders cut living willow roots; her retaliation now threatens the entire worksite.
Exactly three battles keep this a local struggle: escape a failed retreat, defend both crews while they gather, then break the guarded root focus and defeat its maker.
The opening earns passage and a current-map rather than an instant victory over the antagonist. The blocked chart route then forces a joint defense; Mares grants field command, withholding the permanent captaincy until the spring is safe.
Water separates routes the merfolk can cross from banks their opponents must defend. Forest growth slows merfolk and favors woodland; harpoons and tidebolts avoid thornshields’ melee reflection. The final focus interrupts future growth and a reserve patrol, giving destruction a concrete tactical purpose.
Two standards let the player place one reserve wave during the defense. Answering that signal also lets the remaining bank watch recognize it in the finale; ordinary survivors, promotions and veterans carry normally. Neither bank is sacrificed by the choice.
The finale requires forward guards, the focus and the Mother all to fall; killing her early cannot bypass the spring’s occupation. An optional cell offers a released reefguard for the battle.
Mares must survive once the joint command begins. The conclusion resolves the spring seizure and Orra’s original ambition: the crews accept one captain, retain the cleared bank and stop cutting the willow’s living roots.
