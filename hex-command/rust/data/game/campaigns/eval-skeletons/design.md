# The Master’s Cistern

Three chapters follow Grave Adept Mira, an already-undead steward, against Thorncaller Vael’s local woodland force. Mira defends her lich master’s property and her own authority; her servants are a skeletal army, not a community of living-minded heroes.

1. The Tollhouse Chest: seize the occupied chest and withdraw Mira with a skeletal servant. A guardian grows thorn cover across the retreat road; coin and observed ward behavior enable the return.
2. The Dry Culvert: defeat the road screen, bargain with Bryn to leave the grove uncut, and open the old service passage. Bryn is a supplemental ally whose survival is optional; costly fox recruitment leaves the main levy skeletal.
3. The Master’s Cistern: use that open passage to flank the invading screen. Destroy the remaining army, or hold a working garrison while the servants clear the intake and forces Vael’s promised withdrawal. Both endings restore estate possession and remove the invasion.
   The finale’s open basin puts a trampling boar, living tree and mobile flankers against the brittle levy; the earned northern culvert remains a way around the front.
   The first battle rewards withdrawal, the second earns a route and agreement, and the finale uses both. Thorn reflection punishes repeated melee strikes; skeletal casters, piercing resistance and riders give different answers to woodland forces.
   The optional finale patrol can surrender after its outrider dies or retreat when its road is taken. These are alternatives within one encounter, not a forced sacrifice. Prisoners remain passive.
   The grove stays beyond the finale map. Mira’s final order ends the grave-soil digging that caused the dispute. Her master keeps the waterworks, but his steward has learned the cost of obeying him blindly.
