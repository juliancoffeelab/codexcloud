# Campaign tactics for both Luna recruitment policies

Both players receive this guide before chapter one. The mixed player may buy
any unlocked type; the restricted player may buy only the designated family.
Both may use authored troops, recalled survivors and earned promotions. This
guide supplies principles, not a coordinate-by-coordinate winning route. Use
the actual observation's prices, unit rules, objectives and legal options.

## Protect the mission, not just the army

Read every goal and required companion before moving. An escort is not a
front-line attacker: move its support first and keep the courier out of the
next enemy phase's reach until a safe path opens. Speed is useful for reaching
the destination, not for running alone into several enemies. You do not have
to clear the map to finish an escort. Conversely, killing a leader does not
complete an unfinished escort, seal or territory goal.

Units cannot walk through occupied hexes, but have no zone of control and do
not intercept attacks. Screens must occupy actual approaches. A unit standing
behind an exposed courier cannot protect it. Check the next phase after moving
the whole group. Available forecasts include reinforcements and recruitment;
a subsequent action makes that forecast stale. A threat total is a conservative
bound, not an assertion that every enemy can hit the target simultaneously.

## Match weapons to the opponent

| Role                    | Useful work                                                         | Limitation                                                                   |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Sword/skeleton          | Cheap melee screens; Blade against unarmored foes                   | Do not chase targets while leaving casters exposed                           |
| Spear/reef guard        | Durable positions; Pierce against beasts                            | Pierce is weak against bone and plate; reef rookies have no Impact club      |
| Living archer/harpooner | Ranged Pierce against beasts and unarmored targets                  | Poor answer to skeletons and armor                                           |
| Bone archer             | Ranged damage against living rookies; no melee retaliation at range | Impact vulnerability and weak melee reply                                    |
| Adept/wavemender        | Ranged Impact against bones and armor                               | Still needs a screen; healing is adjacent, strongest-only and does not stack |
| Scout                   | Capture, flanking, ranged Impact sling without melee retaliation    | Low damage; courier scouts must survive, not trade themselves for a hit      |
| Boar                    | Durable melee pressure                                              | Slow, vulnerable to Pierce, no ranged reply                                  |
| Wolf                    | Fast flanks and occupation of open approaches                       | Exposed to spears and bows; not a substitute for a durable screen            |
| Thorncaller             | Woodland ranged pressure and later adjacent healing                 | Pierce is weak against bones; use its staff cautiously                       |

Inspect damage previews and the selected weapon. Choose ranged hits on melee
targets to avoid melee retaliation; check ranged replies from bowmen and
casters. Finish a dangerous target with coordinated attacks when that removes
more incoming damage than scattered wounds would. Avoid moving before an
attack that leaves the attacker trapped: attacking ends its movement.

## Keep useful survivors

Buy support early enough to arrive before the battle rather than accumulating
gold while the starting company fights alone. Keep the recruiting leader near
its keep until the needed recruits are deployed; recruiting does not consume
its action. Use a mobile unit to take safe villages for income and healing.
Rotate wounded screens out, bring fresh ones in, and use owned villages or
groves to rest. Retaliation can be lethal: previews matter for both sides.

Promote ready survivors on a fresh turn. Promotion preserves wounds and spends
the unit's turn; it is not an instant full heal. Recall up to four earned units
that fit the next chapter's opponents, not just the four with the most HP.
Veteran ranged support can be worth more than another rookie melee body.

## Campaign priorities

- **Lantern Road:** escort Ivo with coordinated support. Scouts supply useful
  ranged Impact before adepts unlock. Later, use adepts against the dead and
  preserve at least one sealing caster for the finale; restricted purchases
  cannot replace an authored adept. Capture and defend all required villages.
- **Green Accord:** boars screen, wolves reach flanks and thorncallers fire from
  woodland. Dain's fate affects later company strength. Preserve Lark and use
  the living allies' Impact against bound dead.
- **Ashen Oath:** escape is the quarry objective; Orin need not duel the office
  leader. Defend refuge ownership, rotating screens, and fetch medicine if safe.
  Nonlethal defensive attacks are allowed; killing Dain or withdrawing men ends
  mediation. Do not leave Dain at one HP in the finale. Adepts answer bones,
  spears answer beasts, bows answer unarmored living enemies. Seal the stones
  to stop their reinforcement waves.
- **Borrowed Tide:** use water routes for merfolk and ordinary plains when on
  land; woodland/hills expose merfolk and marsh protects them. Mix a durable
  reef screen, Impact support and ranged/mobile harassment when allowed. The
  wreck requires three actual defended enemy phases and high tide; recapture
  resets progress. Both sluice controls and Edda's beacon escort are required.

If a position seems blocked, inspect alternate paths, healing, unused recruits
and attacks before ending another empty turn. A run can remain unfinished:
report its actual state rather than manufacturing a defeat or claiming that
one failed strategy proves the mission impossible.
