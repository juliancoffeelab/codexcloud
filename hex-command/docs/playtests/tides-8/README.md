# Eighth coastal comparison

Both full Luna campaigns replay exactly against frozen source
`deae30cfd83bab3334e442facb5e7ed2dc2e9364`. This includes eight-HP wavemenders,
eleven-HP tide sages and the actual three-phase evacuation hold. New purchases
are unrestricted for mixed recruitment and restricted to reef guards for its
control. Both retain their authored company and earned recalls/promotions.

| Recruitment     | Chapter attempts                      | Casualties across all attempts                    | Retries | Final named survivors |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | ------- | --------------------- |
| Mixed           | 8 / 6 / 5, all won                    | 2 ordinary troops                                 | 0       | Mira, Edda, Tomas     |
| Reef guard only | Opening lost at 2, then won 9 / 6 / 7 | 3 ordinary troops plus Edda in the failed opening | 1       | Mira, Edda, Tomas     |

Mixed recruitment was faster in the opening and finale, lost fewer troops and
needed no retry. Its opening bought reef guards and a wavemender; later chapters
added adepts and swordsmen alongside marine screens. Promotions included a
trident guard, tide sage, tide hunters and Tomas's warden. The restricted army
still used authored ranged and healing allies, earning their promotions; its
successful purchases were seven reef guards, plus one in the failed attempt.

The control's first Edda death followed an exposed advance, then moving her
at one HP into another reachable attack. This was an avoidable player decision,
not evidence that reef recruitment necessarily causes that defeat. Retry
restored its incoming checkpoint, so Edda's later survival does not erase the
earlier failed attempt.

These 135 mixed and 156 restricted decisions support a better recorded mixed
campaign outcome. They do not isolate the healer durability change, establish
optimal play or prove causation from the revision. An independent reviewer
replayed both records, confirmed the casualty/retry counts and advised against
further coastal tuning based on this pair alone. Another independent pair tests
the current source without reading these strategies.
