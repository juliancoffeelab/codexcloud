# Fifth balance comparison

These four Luna trials use frozen commit `06399391d297943259c172a773f7776815a121f7`.
Every recorded action and enemy response replays exactly against that checkout.
Both recruitment policies may use their authored starting units; the restricted
policy buys only its specified unit family. These are individual runs, not a
statistical estimate of optimal play.

| Campaign | Recruitment | Chapter turns, including failed attempts | Casualties | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Green Accord | Mixed | 24 / 4 lost + 5 won / 6 | 14 | Completed with Dain alive; Tamsin lost |
| Green Accord | Boar only | 13 / 4 / 3 lost + 6 won | 7 | Completed; Dain lost |
| Lantern Road | Mixed | 5 / 5 / 7 | 3 | Completed without a retry |
| Lantern Road | Swordsman only | 3 / 5 / ongoing at 15 | 12 | Stopped after losing its only eligible stone-sealing mage |

The Lantern restricted trial is not an engine defeat. Its winner remains null.
The normal game permits replacing the lost mage by recruiting an adept; the
trial's recruitment restriction forbids that action. The transcript preserves
the unfinished state rather than inventing a defeat or erasing the casualty.

Woodland mixed recruitment remained less efficient and suffered twice the
attrition of boar recruitment. This prompted a subsequent focused change:
thorncallers and briarweavers gain additional ranged cover in forest and grove,
while retaining fragile melee and open-ground defenses. A fresh matched pair
tests that change; these results do not claim to validate it.

The Lantern mixed army completed all three chapters with fewer losses and kept
replacement adept capacity. These trials predate the adept price reduction from
10 to 8 gold. They establish the recorded earlier outcome, not a replay of the
later price revision. See `results.json` for individual attacks, recruits,
promotions and casualties, and the accompanying trial files for all decisions.
