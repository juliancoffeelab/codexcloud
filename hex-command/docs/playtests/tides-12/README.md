# Twelfth cohort: stronger bone bows

Frozen source: `06e2352163bade7395db2d6214c83f01e06cd7f9`.
Rookie bone bows deal four damage and veteran bows five. This also changes
enemy bows. Both purchase policies receive the shared strategy guide before
starting, including early recruitment and protection of required companions.
They may retain authored supports and earned recalls; restrictions apply to
new purchases. One retry follows an actual defeat, with a thirty-turn cap.

The interrupted control has no refuge winner. Its checkpoint is an actual
record, not an engine failure or a campaign completion. Mixed loses Orin in
both attempts. These records do not validate an Ashen mixed advantage or
isolate the effect of the bow change from tactical decisions.

The Lantern control bought nineteen swordsmen across its two chapters and
earned two Soldier promotions. At the village cap, Ivo remained alive with
eight HP, alongside twenty other player units against nine enemies. Its
prolonged staging did not achieve the capture objective; this is neither a
victory nor evidence that the enemy outnumbers the recruited army.

The mixed Lantern record also caps at thirty with no village held and a null
winner. Its ferry victory is slower but casualty-free; the village chapter
loses six units, buys five adepts, three swordsmen and a scout, and makes
nineteen attacks. One southern village is captured temporarily, then reclaimed.
Both Lantern policies remain incomplete, and neither result validates balance.
All three villages must be held together; a previous capture is not victory.
