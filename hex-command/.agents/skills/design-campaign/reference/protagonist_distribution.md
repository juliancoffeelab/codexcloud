# Protagonist distribution

Reference sample: **19 distinct authored campaigns**, with one focal player character (“I”) and their player faction (“we”) per campaign. These are two views of the same 19 stories, not 38 independent samples. Supporting characters are not extra entries. Counts describe observed structures and pursuits; use them as a rough reference when designing protagonists.

## Pursuits by kind and role

- **Primary goal:** an organizing desired end.
- **Auxiliary goal:** an additional desired end alongside the main pursuit.
- **Means:** something pursued because it enables an end.

A cell counts one **campaign × kind × role** occurrence. Repeated acquisition of allies within one campaign counts once. A character whose purpose changes can contribute several primary kinds; a kind can serve different roles in different phases. Personal and faction goals can coincide. These counts therefore describe 80 classified occurrences, not 80 characters or mutually exclusive campaign motives.

These count distinct kinds across the campaign, not individual tasks, steps or means needed in every chapter. Repeated acquisition of the same kind counts once per campaign.

For generation, use this table to choose how many means kinds to develop, and the pursuit table’s Means column to choose which kinds. The count and kind distributions are separate; their combinations still need to make sense for the chosen goal.

## Kind distinctions

The [enemy motive reference](motive_distribution.md) defines the eight shared kinds: conquest/subjugation, retaining rule, seizing leadership, wealth, magical power/knowledge, revenge, defense and captives/servants/bodies. Additional kinds here are:

- **Win freedom / independence:** end subjection or outside control, without necessarily taking the ruler’s office.
- **Find / establish a new home:** obtain a viable homeland or hunting domain. Conquest can serve this end.
- **Explore / discover / uncover truth:** understand the world or resolve a mystery, distinct from specifically gaining magical capability.
- **Gain recognition / acceptance:** obtain approval, fame, status, vindication or renewed belonging.
- **Create an artifact:** make and preserve the artifact.
- **Preserve / extend own life:** pursue survival or immortality as an organizing end; a leader-death defeat condition alone does not establish it.
- **Gain forces / allies:** obtain troops, fighting assets or military/political cooperation. Already commanding an army does not by itself establish this pursuit.
- **Gain expertise:** obtain instruction, advice, specialist services or a research partner. This does not automatically include every use of knowledge or magic.

Five campaigns seek both forces/allies and expertise. Their 23 occurrences cover 18 campaigns; separating these kinds changes the number of classified occurrences, not the number of campaigns.

## Reading the tables

For backgrounds or factions, divide by 19 for the category’s share of campaigns. For pursuits, divide a cell by its column total to compare kinds within a role; divide by its row total to see how that kind is used. Each normalization totals 100% in its own direction before rounding.

Pursuit counts are not per-character probabilities: several kinds and roles can occur in one story. Faction identity, character background and motives are separate axes; these marginal distributions do not establish which combinations occur together.
