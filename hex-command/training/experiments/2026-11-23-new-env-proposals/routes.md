# Scree Relay

**Priority** High as a route-transfer candidate. This is a proposed simulation fixture with two paired starts. Geometry, force balance, deadlines and reference feasibility are provisional.

## Source behavior

Echo Gate and North of Lantern Road both went 0/8 at hour10 against Classic. The matched references went 8/8 each. Echo Gate used Full as player reference, with clear sight and wins on turn 6. North of Lantern Road used Queued, with player-only returning fog and wins on turns 5–7. All 16 learner games expired at the authored deadlines on turns 10 and 9 respectively. Those records contain outcomes only. [Fresh audit](../../../training/experiments/2026-11-23-targeted-curriculum-audit/README.md)

The earlier matched hour9 trace supplies the decision evidence. In Echo Gate (seed 27182201), both rangers started turn 2 at (8,2). The learner attacked the new gallery force, lost the ranger by turn 3, and then lost its commander. Full withdrew the ranger immediately, used the bypass, and finished on turn 6. On North of Lantern Road (same evaluation seed), the learner took the officer west for five turns while its escorts took the eastern path. Queued held the officer behind an escort screen for a turn, then moved through the eastern road and finished on turn 6. [Route traces](../targeted-eight-20261122/REPORT.md#commit-to-a-route)

## Proposed encounter

An 18×11 surface map has two eastbound lanes through a band of rough terrain. The direct road follows row 4 and the lower track follows row 7. A short connector near column 6 links them midway, and they merge again near (14,4). Make the lower route roughly two swordsman moves longer from the connector. Use impassable terrain between lanes and a widened rally area at their merge. A ranger has to reach the relay at (14,4), which permanently makes the captain's exit at (16,4) available. The captain must arrive alive before turn 9. Give the objective order and both marks up front. Use the existing named `goTo` and `setFlag` events and current objective rewards. Both actors remain in play.

Start from a position analogous to the turn-2 Echo Gate decision. Put the 22 HP ranger around (8,4), the full-health commander/captain around (4,4), and a full-health swordsman at the connector near (6,4). The ranger is already ahead, with a retreat through the connector still possible. Preserve the split and starting health from the source trace. Use a dreadguard, two impact casters and a bonearcher as the initial enemy roster. Use active Classic enemies from the first phase and zero recruitment for both sides. Start with clear sight to isolate movement and threat response. Run the short starts at the decision point, then check a played-through opening one turn earlier. In that opening, stage the threat just off the direct road so the first player phase can use the road safely and ordinary enemy movement can close it before the captain enters. The earlier opening preserves action history that a restaged snapshot drops.

- **Switch routes.** Place the dreadguard and casters on the north road near columns 9–11 in the short start. The archer pressures the relay approach. The intended successful sequence is a brief ranger withdrawal, a screened crossover for the captain, then a renewed advance on the lower track. The captain has to follow through once the ranger completes the relay.
- **Stay on the road.** Keep the layout, composition and deadline. Put the dreadguard and one caster on the lower track, leaving only a lone caster to threaten the road and the same archer at the far approach. A short wait behind the swordsman while it handles the caster should make the direct road viable. The crossover exposes the captain to the stronger force and spends scarce turns. This is the control for indiscriminate reversal.

Echo Gate tests escaping one gallery and reaching a beacon with any unit. North of Lantern Road tests carrying one officer around a ridge. Scree Relay tests a shorter mid-route switch, keeping the two mission-critical units on a coherent plan, and finishing with the officer after the ranger reaches the relay. The paired control makes it possible to reject a policy that simply attacks every contact or abandons every exposed lane.

## Validation before use

- First smoke-test legal path lengths and the proposed threat sequence under normal enemy play. Require distinct reference trajectories on the exact same objective, roster, visibility and deadline as the learner. Confirm the objectives trigger in order and neither lane allows a direct shortcut to the exit. Select Full or Queued on the initial cohort. If neither works, use an explicitly documented legal-action reference with observed threats and path costs and confirm its feasibility independently.
- Compare hour8 and hour10 to the selected reference on at least 20 matched fresh seeds per start, then confirm on another 20. Report relay activation, living captain arrivals, losses and deadline failures. Log first lane and switch timing, zero-progress turns measured by legal route cost, and turns between relay capture and captain exit. Keep the adverse start only if hour10 reproduces the intended error and the reference succeeds, and keep the control only if reference traces establish why holding or continuing works.
- Withhold a separate geometry with the connector on the opposite side of a staggered ravine and alternate positions for the same threats. Reserve it for any later transfer check along with fresh cases on the original Echo Gate and North of Lantern Road. If any variant uses fog, test it as a separate matched condition and specify which side receives it. Preserve the observed branch and actor decisions alongside completion.
