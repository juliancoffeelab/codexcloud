# Commander coordination proposals

Prioritize **Commander Left Behind** for an exit under live pressure and **Two Banks Under Pressure** for the same survival decision in ordinary combat. Both start with useful allies still alive. Neither needs forced movement, stationary enemies, a protected escort, or reward changes. The dimensions, rosters and clocks below are starting specifications for calibration.

## 1. Commander Left Behind

- **Map and forces.** A 17×12 campaign encounter with a western forest spur, a straight two-bank crossing along row 6 and a longer dry southern route along row 10. A rocky outcrop screens a friendly healing village at (2,7) from the northwestern approach. Nera is a 25 HP swordsman leader at (2,4). An adept at (4,5), archer at (5,5) and scout at (6,7) retain the 17/13/11 HP and the front-to-rear order from `480000010` turn 5. Two active 16 HP skeletons start near the enemy-owned post at (13,6) and an active 10 HP revenant starts at (0,1). The revenant represents the later promoted pursuer in an earlier decision window. The new map changes terrain, positions and enemy timing. Begin with clear vision and zero recruitment on both sides.
- **Authored win.** Reach the exit (15,6) with Nera alive and friendly ownership of the post (13,6) at arrival, using ordinary stand and ownership conditions. Aim for defeat at the start of turn 13, contingent on route witnesses. Any ally can secure the post. The post and exit are public from turn one. Nera can cross with the force, an ally can stay back to screen her, or the company can use the longer flank. Leader loss is defeat. No enemy gets a guard or stationary order.
- **Behavior to expose.** See if the learner sends the fragile forward units into the two skeletons while the healthy commander remains out of useful support range until the revenant closes. Compare with a timely link-up or a genuine screening maneuver. The simultaneous live post and exit objective allows the army to share the work. The solved escorts use a remembered safe-arrival flag before a separate exit unlocks. Flint Switchback and North of Lantern Road emphasize choosing a traversable route. Here the routes are legible and the decision is when and with whom to cross. Rally at the Vault allows any unit to arrive amid a much larger battle and economy. [Existing targeted environment report](../targeted-eight-20261122/REPORT.md)
- **Withdrawal counterpart.** Start Nera at 7 HP with the same map and roster. The friendly village and southern route provide room to pull back while allies screen the approach. Establish a witnessed win with retreat or a protected wait to heal before crossing. Use a matched legal forward-first intervention to test whether immediate follow-through is worse at 7 HP. If the pursuer can cut off the refuge before any healing or the same opening works equally well at both HP levels, adjust the layout and rerun both variants. Treat healing and pressure as hypotheses until the paths and exchanges are recorded.

## 2. Two Banks Under Pressure

- **Map and forces.** A 15×11 fixed-roster deathmatch arena with forest on the west bank, a narrow ford on row 5, a longer dry crossing along row 8 and open eastern ground. A friendly village at (3,7) offers a fallback from the ford. Nera starts at (3,5) at 25 HP, an adept at (4,5) at 12 HP and a crossbowman at (10,5) at 2 HP. An active 13 HP giant spider starts at (6,5), a 16 HP spider at (11,6) and a 22 HP revenant leader at (13,5). This restages the surviving Nera, adept and spiders from `480000018` turn 20 with its endangered crossbowman retained from turn 14. The revenant leader gives the restored fight the standard arena objective. Use authored scenario forces, no recruitment for this isolation pass and clear vision initially. [Arena objective and fixed-force setup](../../../rust/src/simulation/matches.rs)
- **Authored win.** Use ordinary deathmatch scoring, leader victory and the normal 100-round cap. There is no scoring hold or commander destination. The player can regroup behind the ford, fight the western spider with both local units or have an ally screen Nera while the other flank pursues the leader. Keep the west spider fast enough to threaten an unsupported Nera and the east fight close enough for help to matter. Count easy eastern leader kills before either bank presents the intended choice as bypasses when deciding whether this fixture qualifies.
- **Behavior to expose.** A stalled commander can leave a nearby ally to absorb the western attack alone. A distant fragile ally can also attract attention that leaves the western pair without mutual support. Look for coordinated local combat or reunification before the ally dies, with less excess exposure and fewer actionable idle phases. This tests commander survival without a bespoke waypoint, a hold clock or a handoff. It complements Commander Left Behind and tests transfer beyond the three solved escort maps. [Hour-10 audit](../../../training/experiments/2026-11-23-targeted-curriculum-audit/README.md)
- **Hold counterpart.** Keep enemy placements and units fixed. Restore the crossbowman to 24 HP and add a 15 HP allied healer at (10,4), approximating the self-sufficient forward force in the successful `480000013` trace. The commander can hold the safer bank and help the adept screen the western spider while the pair handles the eastern engagement. Compare a covered hold with early leader exposure across the ford. Accept the counterpart only if normal play produces credible hold wins under active pressure and the across-ford opening adds measurable risk. The purpose is to distinguish waiting in support from abandonment, including a winning case with no early commander movement.

## Qualification

1. Reproduce the archived cases with their frozen catalog before interpreting discarded moves. Capture exact decision boundaries if testing a counterfactual and verify the unmodified path first. Preserve fog, combat RNG, faction awareness and recurrent planner history where available. Disclose any fresh episode reset of turn/action history, promotions, flags or visibility. Use the Lantern sources for diagnosis and design. Report fresh full Lantern Road seeds as transfer on a geographically related benchmark, and confirm a distinct layout before claiming layout generalization.
2. Check every final layout in an ignored catalog through actual native games. Verify ordinary authored objectives, passability and phase ordering. Save successful paths, unsuccessful paths, threat ranges and legal alternatives. Confirm the pursuer engages in the tested cell and check whether a route or ally-support intervention changes the outcome. Rerun the exit candidate with a longer clock to see whether the same decisions still fail.
3. Pair the exact current hour-10 checkpoint against a predeclared Classic, Full or Queued player with matched recruiters, opponent, roster, visibility, initiative, deadline and seed. Screen the campaign initially against Predictor and the arena against Full, matching the chosen source enemy planners. Benchmark the historical hour-9 model as a separate comparison if desired. Run at least 20 discovery seeds and a disjoint 20-seed confirmation cohort for each selected cell after any content change, extending if a gap is unclear. The prior targeted report used at least 10 reference wins and an eight-win advantage on 20 games as its qualification screen. If rule-based references cannot demonstrate the decision, a disclosed controller can choose legal actions from public objectives and visible threat/support forecasts. Its treatment of the wounded or hold counterpart must also be verified. Use no scripted action in observed learner or reference games.
4. Require the performance gap and intended failure in the same final cell. Report leader losses, deadlines or caps, success with leader alive, allies lost before/after separation, first threat arrival, path-cost support distance, legal opportunities to screen or rejoin, and remaining distance to exit where applicable. For the counterparts, compare actual healing and pressure before later progress, or pressure absorbed while the forward force succeeds. A useful hold or retreat can retain or increase path distance. Use separately frozen shared-prefix interventions to distinguish changes in the commander decision from changes in the entire policy. Record both sides' visibility when expanding to player-only returning fog. Keep greedy and sampled learned play comparisons separately disclosed.

This proposal used archived traces, authored rules and existing fixture inventories. No new environment or replay was run, and none of the proposed layouts or counterpart outcomes have been validated.
