# Two targeted combat encounter proposals

Both are provisional one-chapter RL scenarios. Use normal campaign objectives and terminal rewards, 0 starting gold, and ordinary enemy turns. Kiln Crossing uses fixed rosters. Juniper Works gives the enemy normal recruitment from an owned keep. All coordinates are zero-based `(column, row)` and all starting units are full HP unless specified. Layouts, starting forces, and target rounds are design starting points. Neither layout has been simulated.

## Evidence

Bellmarket Courts and Thorn Verge cleared 32/32 and 72/72 fresh hour10 games. Both end on killing a single wounded ranged captain, and both start five player units against two enemies without a dedicated enemy melee screen. Lair Outworks instead requires eliminating three ranged units. It cleared 18/72, split 17/24 in full vision, 1/24 in player-side fog, and 0/24 in both-sides fog. Its 54 defeats all ran out the authored deadline. These results show a fog gap on the authored layout. The audit did not record state transitions that establish how the player lost time. [Audit](../../../training/experiments/2026-11-23-targeted-curriculum-audit/README.md)

A small matched probe used the archived native binding and frozen run catalog to replace only the player planner with the Full rule-based planner. Against Classic, hour10 versus Full was 5/8 versus 7/8 in full vision (seeds 490000425–432), 0/8 versus 6/8 in player-side fog (490000449–456), and 0/8 versus 6/8 in both-sides fog (490000505–512). Results are specific to Classic on this layout. [Raw matched results](lair-classic-reference.json)

The four traced Lantern crossing regressions cleared that chapter at hour8 and ended in commander loss at hour9 after the rest of the army had died. In two traces the commander remained near her starting position for most of the chapter. In the other two she fell after trying to retreat from revenants. Hour10 cleared one, capped two, and lost the commander on the fourth. The hour10 rerun recorded outcomes only. This motivates a bounded tactical decision before the army collapses. ## Kiln Crossing

**Decision.** Scout a ranged battery through fog, stage the melee line, and renew contact with a flank that the line can support. Four enemy melee units have enough attack output to punish a head-on rush. The battery has two meaningful targets and both must fall.

- **Layout.** 11 columns × 8 rows, mostly plains. The ridge runs down column 5, with mountain hexes in rows 0, 2, 5, 6 and 7. The cover route goes through hill `(5,1)`. The exposed road crosses through `(4,3)`, `(5,3)`, `(6,3)` and the parallel row 4. Ruins at `(4,4)` provide cover on the near approach. Forest at `(4,1)` supports observation. Forest at `(6,1)` and `(7,0)` provides a staging shelf east of the ridge, offset from the road. Ranged line-of-fire checks must include both possible intervening hexes.
- **Player.** `commander` (leader) `(3,4)`, `soldier` `(4,3)`, `hammerman` `(3,3)`, `swordsman` `(3,2)`, `archer` `(3,5)`, `scout` `(4,1)`.
- **Enemy.** `skeleton-dreadguard` `(6,3)`, `skeleton-axeman-l2` `(6,2)`, `revenant` `(7,4)`, `skeleton` `(7,5)`, `deatharcher` `(7,1)`, `skeleton-impact-caster-l2` `(8,3)`. Battery weapons have 23 combined nominal ranged damage to the player's 8 before terrain, armour and randomness. The enemy's four dedicated melee units have less aggregate HP, higher nominal damage, and a better starting position than the player's four.
- **Objective.** Defeat both battery crews before the start of player round 9 and keep the commander alive. Use the authored `noUnits` selector with the two unit types and a matching visible defeat objective. Keep the normal attack and movement rules for the melee screen. For the primary variant, apply radius-3 fog to both sides using the existing concealment route pattern. Pair it with full vision to isolate fog. The archer starts three hexes from the scout and can break contact by moving east. A scout move or withdrawal can also change contact, depending on the rest of the line's position. Qualify the layout only if legal play actually yields meaningful contact changes.
- **Expected split.** The learner may attack the first visible enemy with only the scout, enter the road piecemeal, or stop progressing once a targeted caster disappears. A reference should trade from road cover or a safe frontage while the scout reacquires the battery from the shelf, then hit from two directions before the screen can shift. A useful forward commander action can finish a melee defender once the soldier has drawn the first attacks. Charging across the initial road gives the screen a close-range fight under both ranged threats. Waiting at the ridge forever loses on time. Holding a turn to enter together is plausible. A scout kill or an exposed target can make immediate commitment correct later.
- **What to observe.** First reveal and subsequent loss of contact for each crew, turn of reacquisition, player infantry within one attack move of the same target when the first soldier enters the road, outgoing damage before first allied melee loss, and remaining time when each battery unit is killed. Trace full-vision and fog cases even when the scores disagree. Verify that the weaker action is a premature assault or stalled reacquisition on the actual trajectories.

## Juniper Works

**Decision.** Rotate a durable line between two threatened earthworks and use the commander when healthy enough to hold the line. The opposing army has two stronger ranged units, four credible melee units and income to replace losses. Hold territory through a sustained attack while their ranged units remain a live threat.

- **Layout.** 10 columns × 8 rows, plains with two initially neutral forts at `(5,4)` and `(6,3)`. Road in `(3,4)`–`(4,4)` and `(6,4)`–`(9,4)` creates the exposed direct approach. Forest at `(4,3)`, `(4,5)`, `(6,2)` and `(6,5)` offers covered staging and retreat. A friendly grove at `(2,5)` provides a covered fallback with existing occupant healing. The enemy owns a keep at `(9,4)` and a village at `(8,6)`. The only victory sites are the forts. Start with full vision so the choice is about combat risk.
- **Player.** `grovewarden` leader `(4,4)` starts at 32/34 HP in the committed-line case and 12/34 HP in the wounded case. `treant` `(4,5)`, `fox-spearguard` `(4,3)`, `greatboar` `(3,4)`, `briarweaver` `(3,5)`. Both cases use the same enemy deployment and geometry. The Treant's piercing resistance, the spearguard's thorns and the forest shoulders give the player tools against the larger force.
- **Enemy.** `soldier` `(6,4)`, `warden` `(7,3)`, `halberdier` `(7,5)`, `swordsman` `(7,2)`, `arbalester` `(8,3)`, `horse-bowman` `(8,5)`. Enemy dedicated melee HP is 121 versus 124 for the four healthy Woodland melee-capable units including their leader. Enemy ranged weapons provide 26 combined nominal damage versus a maximum of 14 from the player's briarweaver and leader. These are roster comparisons before armour, terrain and retaliation. A `commander` leads from the keep at `(9,4)` with legal `swordsman` and `archer` recruits. The village supplies 2 gold per round, enough for a seven-gold recruit roughly every four rounds if it stays owned. Use the default objective defence guidance and let opponents contest both forts with ordinary actions. Confirm the recruiter actually replenishes troops and that the fight reaches the forts throughout the hold.
- **Objective.** Control and occupy both forts with friendly troops for ten consecutive round ticks while keeping the commander alive. Provisional defeat starts on player round 19. Use concurrent existing ownership and `held` predicates with explicit player faction and list both hold objectives in the mission. Verify event tick semantics, that rotations work across phase boundaries, and that either fort lost or unstaffed breaks the qualifying interval. The reference should face credible counterattacks during that interval, including pressure in its later rounds.
- **Expected split.** The learner may leave the leader at the back while the same two infantry trade away their HP, or place the wounded leader on an open fort under combined melee and bow reach. A healthy grovewarden can fight from a shoulder or step into a vacated safe slot after the Treant absorbs fire. In the wounded case the same commitment should be inferior to falling back behind the line for healing and rejoining later. Even a healthy leader should avoid chasing the mounted bow out of the forts' defensive envelope. The counterfactual to test is whether screened participation improves the healthy case and withdrawal improves the wounded case, using ordinary legal commands in both.
- **What to observe.** Overlap of hold progress, leader and nonleader participation before the first loss, high-exposure turns and HP at the leader's first retreat or attack, pressure from each enemy weapon, whether the force recaptures a fort after rotation, actual enemy spending and arrivals at the front, and how many hold rounds saw enemy attacks. Discard the layout if the last several hold rounds routinely pass without opposition or the enemy line predictably overwhelms the reference. Verify that a stationary leader can reasonably lose with otherwise competent play. Leader loss ends the encounter.

## Qualification before curriculum consideration

Build a disposable catalog snapshot under ignored `tmp/rl/` for any later implementation and preserve the experiment's native inference configuration, normal objectives and reward semantics. Hash the catalog, model files and native binding. Start with 8 matched tuning seeds per case using greedy hour10, greedy hour8, and the Full rule-based planner, with Classic opposition fixed. The checkpoints are archived under `training/experiments/2026-11-23-ppo-5m-9h/recovery/hour-08/` and `extension-one-hour/recovery/hour-10/`. Reference, learned checkpoints and any tactical rule alternatives receive the same faction-scoped observations, map, visible objective data and action legality. A rule can track its own legal observations across time. Ground truth may be used in replay analysis. If Full fails because it lacks the required coordination, permit an observation-scoped tactical reference and report which reference generated each result.

After tuning, freeze the layouts and run 24 fresh paired seeds per accepted condition, first against Classic and then across a smaller independent Predictor or Full opposition check. Keep tuning and confirmation seeds separate. As a provisional acceptance bar for each critical case, require at least 18/24 reference clears and a reference lead of at least 6/24 over hour10, with replay evidence of the intended decision in both directions. Keep hour8 results visible so a regression can be distinguished from a weakness shared by both checkpoints. If the counterfactual case flips the better action, demonstrate the flip by replaying the first meaningful policy branch with shared initial seeds and normal combat resolution. Record objective progress, end reason, enacted paths, current visibility and casualties. Check that results persist if the authored deadline is relaxed by one round and that there is no universal hold, charge, or static leader line that trivializes the pair. If both learned checkpoints already succeed or the intended mechanism is missing, revise or drop the condition. A proposal can be winnable with a competent reference and still lack evidence that it teaches the intended choice.
