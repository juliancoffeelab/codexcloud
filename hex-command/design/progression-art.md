# Visual progression specifications

All coordinates refer to the editable 48×48 matrices. The approved family pose
is the starting point, not a raster to resize. Light comes from above left;
human, undead and merfolk faces look toward their weapons on screen right.
Standing humanoids retain their existing approximately 36–41-cell body height;
the scout remains visibly crouched. Weapons, banners, tails and cloaks may extend
beyond the body. Faction pairs share anatomy and equipment geometry.

Each material uses a flat base and a connected shadow, with a highlight only
where it describes a surface. Ivory in a cloak, gold outside trim, or an isolated
material speck without a component is a defect even if present in the palette.
No interpolated colors, noise, glow, resampling or scattered rank decorations.

Before each family is edited, verify its actual symbols and component boundaries.
Render directly from the edited matrices; inspect both ranks and both factions
together on dark and green ground. A full roster sheet checks body scale and
detail consistency. Independent review should find defects against the approved
sources and these component descriptions, rather than accepting palette or
source-to-atlas equality as proof of visual correctness.
