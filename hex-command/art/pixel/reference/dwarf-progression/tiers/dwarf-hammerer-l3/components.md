# dwarf-hammerer-l3

L3 artwork derived from the cleaned `dwarf-hammerer` pose. The branch assignment is in `art/pixel/lineups/dwarves.json`.

Armour: L1 leather; L2 metal helmet, shoulders, cuirass and arm guards over leather leggings, boots and apron; L3 complete silver coverage. Face, hands, beard, wooden shafts, weapon steel, faction fabric and mount/tack remain their own materials.

The exact native inputs and ownership masks are saved under `art/pixel/reference/dwarf-progression/bases/dwarf-hammerer/`. Edit that recipe and run `python3 art/pixel/reference/dwarf-progression/build.py`; do not patch PNGs or reuse the older armour rectangles.
