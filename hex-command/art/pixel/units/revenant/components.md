# revenant

Veteran of skeleton. The selected face-guard helmet retains the approved
jagged crown and open eye sockets. Three broken points join the dome at
x26–34/y4–10; cheek plates and a narrow nose guard descend through y11–14.
The exposed bone teeth, rib gaps, bone hands and two connected legs remain.
Helmet shading follows upper-left light: connected `A` planes cover the left
dome and near cheek, with one connected `S` upper-left highlight. The far dome,
far cheek, underside and brow rim use `a`. The central nose ridge uses a
continuous `A` plane with an `a` lower tip. The right crown point is shadowed;
the near cheek has no alternating light/dark checkerboard cells.
The level-one skeleton remains bareheaded. Grey steel `A/a` forms the shoulder guard at x18–22,y17–19, plates on
the exposed shins above the boots, and the shield rim at x35–46,y18–34. Bone
hands, shield boss and diagonal sword blade retain their original materials.
Gold `G/g` now belongs to the connected sword guard at x15–17,y29–32 as well as
the belt buckle. Both factions share anatomy and equipment. Light is upper left.

# Skeleton warrior: option B

The selected reference is the left warrior in
`art/pixel/reference/skeleton-wolf/option-B.png`. Both faction sprites use the
same 48×48 geometry, with teal cloth/shield changing to crimson.

Components are an adult skull in three-quarter view toward the right, exposed
rib groups and sternum, a pelvis, separated articulated bone arms and legs,
bone hands, a sword held low across the front toward the right, and a compact
shield covering the shield-side arm. A short scarf and torn cape stream left;
tattered cloth hangs from the leather belt. Compact leather boots meet the
bottom baseline.

Bone uses warm ivory and one shaded ivory; the sockets and spaces between ribs
use the dark outline. Sword blade and guard use grey steel with a lighter edge.
The shield has a brown rim, faction cloth face and grey boss. Brown leather
forms the wrist guards, belt, shoulder strap and boots; gold belongs to the
belt buckle and veteran sword guard. Cloth uses three flat faction shades.
Light falls from upper left. The veteran shield rim and shin/shoulder plates
replace the beginner leather/bone surfaces with connected steel planes.

The silhouette has a one-pixel dark contour around its original opaque pixels.
This contour separates ivory limbs, sword and shield from the green map ground;
it does not move the interior pixels or their attachment points.

The skull, hands, ribs, joints, sword grip and shield boss must remain legible
as deliberate pixel clusters. The sword is a single connected weapon. Its
blade passes in front of the thighs without replacing their anatomy. The
shield-side hand connects to the shield; the other hand grips the sword.
