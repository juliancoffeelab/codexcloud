# Skeleton warrior: option B

The selected reference is the left warrior in
`art/pixel/reference/skeleton-wolf/option-B.png`. Both faction sprites use the
same 48×48 geometry, with teal cloth/shield changing to crimson.

Components are an adult skull in three-quarter view toward the right, exposed
rib groups and sternum, a pelvis, separated articulated bone arms and legs,
bone hands, a sword held low across the front toward the right, and a compact
shield covering the shield-side arm. A short scarf and torn cape stream left;
tattered cloth hangs from the leather belt. Compact leather boots meet the
bottom baseline.

Bone uses warm ivory and one shaded ivory; the sockets and spaces between ribs
use the dark outline. Sword blade and guard use grey steel with a lighter edge.
The shield has a brown rim, faction cloth face and grey boss. Brown leather
forms the wrist guards, belt, shoulder strap and boots; gold is confined to the
belt buckle. Cloth uses three flat faction shades. Light falls from upper left.

The skull, hands, ribs, joints, sword grip and shield boss must remain legible
as deliberate pixel clusters. The sword is a single connected weapon. Its
blade passes in front of the thighs without replacing their anatomy. The
shield-side hand connects to the shield; the other hand grips the sword.
