# Death archer — level two skeleton archer

Uses the same larger anatomy as Bone archer: raised skull/shoulders, slightly
broader skull/rib edge and complete lengthened thigh/shin chains, with boots
grounded at row 47. The face, bone hands, rib gaps and braced stance remain readable.

Components: right-facing ivory skull, drawing hand at the nock, extended bow arm
and gripping fingers, rib groups/legs, leather belt/boots, faction scarf/cloak,
steel shoulder/wrist guards, larger reinforced wood bow and a centered thin arrow.

The compact leather quiver sits diagonally behind the screen-left shoulder.
Two narrow wooden arrow shafts with ivory fletching project from its opening.
The case/rim and shoulder strap remain attached, with the drawing arm occluding
their lower portion. The quiver does not replace a hand or obscure the ribs.

The bow tips are (34,6) and (34,40), meeting the string at nock (24,23).
The one-row arrow lies at the midpoint y23; its five-cell head occupies rows
22–24. Steel bindings reinforce the upper and lower wooden bow limbs.

Materials: ivory bone B/b, leather L/l, steel A/a, wood W/w, silver S/s,
faction cloth q/c/C or r/R/h and gold G/g. Upper-left illumination;
skull and weapon point screen right. Both factions share all geometry.
