# Dire wolf

The approved base wolf supplies the complete pose, four legs, tail, ears,
compact closed muzzle and attached fang. Copy that anatomy exactly.

Add swept back fur at x20–34/y23–27. Its upper `H` fur plane and lower
`D` shadow join the existing shoulder and neck behind the faction collar.
The back fur tapers toward the rump and follows the body's contour.

Face screen right; light falls from the upper left. The inherited palette uses
`D/H` for charcoal coat, `F/G` for grey fur patches, `O` for the outline,
nose, brow and mouth, and `I` for the fang. `T/t` and `R/r` are faction
cloth. Keep the original connected material planes and hard transparency.

The selected veteran coat is nearly black, with cool charcoal highlights and
dark grey muzzle, belly and leg patches. These colours preserve the original
shading regions, facial features and faction cloth. Dark and white studies
use this exact matrix; the dark study is the version used by the game.
