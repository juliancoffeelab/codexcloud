# Horse archer

Finished level-1 human artwork on a 64×64 native canvas.
Both factions share the same silhouette; cloth carries faction color while
equipment and body colors retain the approved design. Each matrix character
maps to its local RGBA palette; `.` is fully transparent. Preserve the complete
canvas when rendering or packing.

Artwork is available independently of gameplay registration.
See [the design workflow](../../reference/human-anatomies/README.md).
