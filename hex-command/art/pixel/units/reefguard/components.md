# Reef guard — level one

Leather cuirass, wooden kite shield, sea-green scales, faction mantle, attached hands and a continuous grounded tail. The geometry derives from the previously reviewed L2 pose; L2 now advances to aquatic coral armour.

The approved generated leather chest and wooden shield material patterns are transferred by `reference/merfolk-materials/build.cjs` using saved baseline matrices. Body, weapon and native silhouette are preserved. The trident uses the reviewed broad three-tined head.

Both faction matrices share anatomy and equipment. Teal/red occurs on faction cloth; skin and equipment retain their material colours.
