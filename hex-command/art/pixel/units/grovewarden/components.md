## Components and materials

`B/b` is the antlers, staff and leather torso/belt/boots. `V/v` is the leaf
cluster on the staff; `S/s` is confined to face and exposed hands. Ivory
sleeves and split undercloth use `C/c`; gold `G/g` belongs to the clasp and
lower cloth hems. `T/t` (enemy `R/r`) is the hood and cloak. `O` separates
components. Gold and ivory do not appear as flecks inside the cloak.
Colors are flat with connected material shadows, no dithering.
