# dwarf-thundersmith-l3

Native rune-line artwork from `art/pixel/reference/rune-line/`. Face, gripping hand, beard and wooden shafts keep their own materials. The pointed apron, torso, overskirt, trousers and boots have independent fill seeds. Thundersmith uses a sun-gold hand crystal and crystal-blue hammer, partial steel L2 armour with leather lower garments, and full silver-and-gold L3 armour. Stonespell uses a heavy slate hood and robes; L3 adds stone shoulders, restrained edging and a crystal-blue rune tablet. Faction cloth swaps teal to red; stone and crystal colours remain shared.

Run `python3 art/pixel/reference/rune-line/build.py` to rebuild, or add `--check` to verify.
