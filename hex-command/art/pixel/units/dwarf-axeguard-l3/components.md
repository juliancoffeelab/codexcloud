# dwarf-axeguard-l3

L3 branch of `dwarf-axeguard`, using the cleaned `dwarf-axeguard` pose reference at the starter's native scale and material palette. Complete detected surfaces are filled. L2 has steel upper armour over leather lower armour; L3 has silver armour with gold hems and faction-cloth embroidery. Faces, hands, beard, weapon wood/steel, mount and tack retain their base materials. Teal cloth becomes red for the enemy; gold is shared. L1 and the rune family are untouched.

Rebuild with `python3 art/pixel/reference/dwarf-upgrades/build.py`; assignments are in that folder's `regions.json` and must match the exact detected base surfaces.
