# dwarf-runesmith

Native rune-line artwork from `art/pixel/reference/rune-line/`. Face, gripping hand, beard, wooden shafts and crystal keep their own materials. The pointed apron, torso, overskirt, trousers and boots have independent fill seeds. Thundersmith uses a crystal-blue hammer and steel L2 / silver-and-gold L3 armour. Stonespell uses green L2 / darker green-and-gold L3 robes and leather boots. Faction cloth swaps teal to red; magic and robe greens remain shared.

Run `python3 art/pixel/reference/rune-line/build.py` to rebuild, or add `--check` to verify.
