# Merfolk mace and shield

Regenerated from old-roster merfolk anchors with a compact upright torso and one connected curled supporting tail. Native skin, hand grips, weapon shafts, net mesh and contours are retained through tiers. Teal cloth alone swaps to red. Rebuild with `npm run art:expansion -- merfolk-maceguard`.

L1 material override: leather armour and wooden shield, superseded by the approved generated-material transfer in `reference/merfolk-materials/build.cjs`. Weapon materials remain unchanged.

Approved generated wooden shield pattern is transferred by `reference/merfolk-materials/build.cjs`. Reef guard also uses generated leather chest panels; Macer retains its previous armour. Saved pre-transfer matrices preserve the body and equipment silhouette.
