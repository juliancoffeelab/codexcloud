# swordsman

48×48 source derived from the approved soldier pose. Beginner infantry has leather headgear, cloth torso and trousers, leather boots, wrists and shield frame. Steel is retained only on the weapon. Both factions share geometry. Ivory cloth has one shadow; brown leather has one shadow; teal/red cloth has one shadow. Light is upper left and the unit faces screen right. Promotion replaces these materials with the existing steel-armored soldier artwork.
