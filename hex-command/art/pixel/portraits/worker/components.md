# Civilian worker portrait

48×48, upright, facing three-quarter right, lit from above left. This portrait
is for civilian speakers, not a recruit or battlefield unit.

Components: straw hat with a broad horizontal brim; brown hair; a skin face
and exposed hands; ivory work shirt and trousers; brown leather belt and boots;
a small wicker basket at the right hip. No cloak, knife, bow or faction badge.

`H/h` is straw, `I/i` ivory cloth, `S/s` skin, `L/l` leather and hair,
`W/w` wicker, `O` outline and `.` transparency. Shadows form broad attached
planes. Basket bars are deliberate connected weave rather than color noise.
The neutral portrait is shared by the artwork styles and packed into the same
public unit/portrait atlas; it has no entry in the game's combat unit roster.
